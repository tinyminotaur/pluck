# Code of Conduct

Be kind, assume good faith, and keep it welcoming. We follow the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Expected:** respectful, constructive discussion; credit for other people's work; patience with newcomers.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised or violent content, publishing someone's
private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may remove content, close threads and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's
private "Report content" option on the item, or open a private security advisory if the issue is sensitive.
